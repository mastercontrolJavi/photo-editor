An offline photo editor for iPhone ProRAW, JPEG, PNG and HEIC. No account, no internet connection.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 11 or newer) | `PhotoEditor-mac.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `PhotoEditor-windows.zip` |

## Install on a Mac

1. Open `PhotoEditor-mac.dmg` and drag **Photo Editor** onto **Applications**.
2. Open **Photo Editor** from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Photo Editor. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only do steps 2 and 3 once. The warning appears because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.

## Install on Windows

1. Unzip `PhotoEditor-windows.zip` and double-click **Photo Editor.exe**.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Full instructions and a guide to the controls are in the README.

The Mac version is built and tested by GitHub's Mac machines. It has not yet been used by a person on a real Mac, so please report anything that looks wrong.
